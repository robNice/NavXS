# NavXS – Google Play Store Listing (English)

## App name (max. 30)

NavXS – Navigation Overlay

## Short description (max. 80)

Floating Back, Home & Recents buttons for the apps you choose.

## Full description (max. 4000)

NavXS shows floating navigation buttons – Back, Home and Recent Apps – on top of the apps you select. It is useful for apps that hide the system navigation, for full-screen apps, or whenever the regular navigation is hard to reach.

**Features**
• Three fixed buttons: Back, Home and Recent Apps
• Choose the apps in which the overlay should appear
• Position each button freely by drag & drop or with precise controls
• Customise size, colour, transparency and theme of every button
• Optional burn-in protection for OLED screens
• Light and dark mode

**Use of the Accessibility Service API**
NavXS requires the Android Accessibility Service to provide its core feature:
• to detect which app is currently in the foreground, so the overlay is only shown in the apps you selected
• to display the floating navigation buttons over those apps
• to perform the actions Back, Home and Recent Apps when you tap a button

NavXS only uses the package name of the foreground app. It does not read typed text, passwords, message content or any other screen content, and it never performs actions on your behalf other than the navigation action of the button you tapped.

Before the service is requested, NavXS explains its use and asks for your consent with the options "Decline" and "Agree". You enable the service yourself in the Android settings and can disable it there at any time.

**Privacy**
• All processing happens locally on your device
• No data is collected, sold or shared
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
